# Building a Second Brain


> Introduction to the PARA method and CODE technique for digital organization


## Content


https://www.buildingasecondbrain.com/

Building a Second Brain: A Proven Method to Organize Your Digital Life and Unlock Your Creative
Potential — Tiago Forte describes the CODE method (Capture, Organise, Distil, Express) and the PARA
system (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) as the backbone of a personal knowledge management
system. The book argues that externalising knowledge into a trusted system frees mental RAM for
creative thinking.
